Enterprise Terms of Service & Operational SLA

1. Authorized Telemetry & Scope of Analysis

CyberTriage is deployed strictly for authorized security assessment, vulnerability triage, and code remediation within systems owned or explicitly authorized by the operating entity. Unauthorized scanning or exploit payload transmission outside defined scopes is prohibited.

2. AI Remediation Verification Mandate

Remediation recommendations, code diffs, and containment rules generated by the AI triage engine must be validated in pre-production staging environments prior to production rollout. The operating organization maintains operational authority over patch deployments.

3. Service Level & Availability

The CyberTriage backend delivers 99.9% uptime for automated finding ingestion and batch reasoning pipelines, with real-time audit logging and deterministic fallback models during upstream API throttling.

Privacy & AI Data Governance Policy

1. Zero Data Retention (ZDR) on Large Language Models

Customer telemetry, stack traces, and scanner outputs processed through the AI Triage Engine are evaluated statelessly. Prompts and completions are not used to train or refine public machine learning foundation models.

2. Automated Telemetry Sanitization & PII Redaction

Raw evidence payloads undergo client and server-side secret filtering before inference. API tokens, private keys, and session cookies identified in raw headers are masked to prevent inadvertent data exposure.

3. Regulatory Alignment

Data handling protocols strictly conform with SOC 2 Type II, ISO/IEC 27001, GDPR Article 32, and HIPAA security rule requirements for protected digital infrastructure telemetry.
